# Tool-holder and organization design grammar

Compose holders from four explicit layers:

| Layer | Options |
|---|---|
| Mounting | French cleat, screw/bolt mount, pegboard adapter, benchtop base |
| Body | back plate, shelf, cheek, spacer, tray, divider, gusset |
| Retention | hole, slot, U-slot, cradle, comb, hook, pocket, lip, tray |
| Information | engraved label, size, generic outline, module number, fit type, orientation |

Prefer functional retention geometry over copied product silhouettes. A shadow-board layout may use a generic outline, name, size, tool number, and position, with an optional contrasting backing layer. Verify reach, sharp-edge exposure, centre of mass, removal clearance, anti-lift behaviour, fastener pull-out, and safe load before shop use.

The modular face system begins at 100 × 100 mm and scales through 450 × 300 mm. Faces are configuration, not a structural rating. Existing pegboard is treated as a possible substrate for approved backers or rail adapters; ordinary hooks are not the organization standard.
