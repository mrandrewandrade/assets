# Combined Teacher Marking

*Past, Present & Becoming + The Way We Meet*

**Technology Commons - Port Credit Secondary School**

## Quick key

**Presentation:** K = Knowledge, T = Thinking, C = Communication, V = Visuals/accessibility, A = Application. Each is /2. Record the presentation total as **P /10**.

**Listening observation flags:**

- **PH** = phone
- **TK** = talking / side conversation
- **OF** = off-task / not facing the speaker
- **NN** = not taking notes
- **PR** = prompted / reminded

Mark a flag only when you observe it. A blank flag means no concern was observed, not that attention was perfect. Complete **L /10** after checking the student's submitted listening-note photos and self-evaluation.

**LS:** Responsibility, Organization, Independent Work, Collaboration, Initiative, Self-Regulation.
