## Learning goal

I can reflect on where I have been, describe who I am now, and share something about who I am becoming in a way that helps others connect with me.

> **Tell us where you have been, where you are, and where you are heading.**

<div class="brief-grid" markdown="1">
<div markdown="1">

## What could you share?

Choose whatever helps us understand you. Some ideas:

- something from your past that shaped you
- a project, experience, skill, or moment you are proud of
- what matters to you now: interests, skills, people, places, or routines
- something others may not know about you
- something you hope to learn, make, fix, try, or become
- a future goal, direction, or possibility you are curious about

You do **not** need to include everything on this list.

</div>
<div class="accessibility-note" markdown="1">

## Keep it accessible

If you use slides, everyone in the room should be able to understand them.

- use large, readable text
- use clear contrast
- keep layouts simple
- use images when they help explain something
- avoid too much text
- do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning

**Fancy slides are not the goal. Clear, accessible communication is.**
