## The goal

Introduce yourself in a way that helps the class get to know you and make connections with you.

You can keep it simple. There is no required number of slides, pictures, or facts.

> **Help us get to know you. Help us connect with you.**

## What could you share?

Choose whatever helps us understand you. Some ideas:

- skills or things you are good at
- hobbies and interests
- past projects or things you have made, fixed, designed, or worked on
- a fun or interesting fact
- future goals
- something you are looking forward to learning, making, fixing, or trying in this class

You do **not** need to include everything on this list.

<div class="accessibility-note">

## Keep it accessible

If you use slides, everyone in the room should be able to understand them.

- use large, readable text
- use clear contrast
- keep layouts simple
- use images when they help explain something
- avoid too much text
- do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning

**Fancy slides are not the goal. Clear, accessible communication is.**

### Going above and beyond

A 4+ might come from a memorable story, a thoughtful connection, an effective project or object, a strong visual, a creative example, or another choice that genuinely helps the audience understand and connect with you.

These are ideas, not extra requirements. **A simple, clear, genuine presentation can earn 100%.**
